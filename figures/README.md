# Figures

**The filenames do not match the printed figure numbers.** LaTeX numbers figures by the order their
environments appear in the source, not by the label text, and in this paper the two diverge. Use this
table, not the filename, when matching a figure to the paper.

| Printed as | File | LaTeX label | Subject |
|---|---|---|---|
| Figure 1 | `fig1_euler_rates` | `fig:1` | Euler x28: energy rates of the perturbation families |
| Figure 2 | `fig2_viscous_rates` | `fig:2` | Navier-Stokes x144: energy rates at two viscosities |
| Figure 3 | `fig3_crossing_vs_nu` | `fig:3` | m = 1 short-wave family: threshold crossing against viscosity |
| **Figure 4** | **`fig6_columnar`** | `fig:6` | the columnar (parallel-flow) spectrum of the front column |
| **Figure 5** | **`fig4_constants`** | `fig:4` | the Type I constants against the amplification label |

The comma-separated data behind Figures 1 to 3 is in `../figdata/`, regenerated by
`../figdata_extract.py`. Figures 4 and 5 are produced from the state table by `../fig4_from_table.py`
and `../make_fig6.py`.

## Not used in the paper

- `fig5_resolution` -- generated by `../make_figures.py` but not included in the paper.
- `fig7_landscape` -- belonged to a superseded combined draft that is no longer in this repository.

Both are kept because the scripts that produce them are shipped; neither appears in `paper/paper1.pdf`.
